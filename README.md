# Echo

WO-I001 · A reference library for Mac.

What you collect becomes direction for what you make.

You save things because they stay with you. A photograph. A page. A post you could not scroll past. Over the years they pile up in folders, and nobody reads them. Not even you.

Echo is a place to keep them, find them again and put them to work.

The Office kept a folder like this for years. It was never once opened on purpose.

## How it works

1. **Capture.** Send a page or an image from your browser. Bring in saved posts from X and Pinterest.
2. **Library.** Find it again by search or by colour. Star it. Write a note on it.
3. **Projects.** Pull references onto a board for the work in front of you.
4. **Echo.** Echo reads what you have kept and writes back one page on what your eye keeps choosing. Paste it into a brief, or into the AI tools you use.


## Your files

Your references stay on your Mac. You do not need an account to use Echo.
